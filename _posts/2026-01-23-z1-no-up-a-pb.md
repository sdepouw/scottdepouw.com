On January 18th 2026 I got a new personal best in the main Zelda speedrunning category! My Any% No Up+A personal best is now `28:48.584`, placing me in [14th on the leaderboard](https://www.speedrun.com/the_legend_of_zelda/runs/z176lxrz){:target="_blank"} as of today!

> Times recorded in LiveSplit are subject to human error. The final actual time is frame counted to produce the more accurate `28:48.584` final time.

This run manipulated Level 3 all the way through, meaning that the run's start was nearly perfect. There is a different manipulation pattern that's a hair faster, but `2:06` is about as good as it gets. A very fast Level 4 meant I was touching that triforce piece at `4:57`, which is an excellent start.

Level 2 was the first real slowdown but the rest of the early game was very fast. Same for the endgaame: Level 8 clocked in at `1:54` (a "good" time for this split is around `1:59`). The final split, Level 9, was `5:02` (fast!), leaving little room for improvement on either end of the run.

The mid-game is where there's time to save, along with various screens in some other parts. Level 2 was a little weaker than normal, along with 5, 7, and 6.

I'm still gunning for `28:2x`!
